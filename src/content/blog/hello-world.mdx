## Live scenes in posts

Posts are written in MDX, so I can drop an interactive Three.js scene straight into the text. Move your cursor
over the arm below:

<SceneCanvas scene="robot-arm" height="360px" />

The arm solves a two-link inverse kinematics problem every frame. With link lengths `L1` and `L2` and a target
at distance `d`, the elbow angle comes from the law of cosines:

```cpp
// elbow interior angle
double gamma = std::acos((L1*L1 + L2*L2 - d*d) / (2.0 * L1 * L2));
double q3 = M_PI - gamma;
```
